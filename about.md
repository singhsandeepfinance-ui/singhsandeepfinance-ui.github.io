## What I work on

<!-- Edit this list to match your current research. -->

- Making finance research useful for everyday investors

## Why this site

Most money advice online is either too thin or too technical. Here I try to sit in between: clear explanations that still show the reasoning, the numbers and, where it helps, the research behind them.
