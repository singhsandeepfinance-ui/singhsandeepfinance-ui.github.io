## Why this site

Most money advice online is either too thin or too technical. Here I try to sit in between: clear explanations that still show the reasoning, the numbers and, where it helps, the research behind them. You'll also find free [money calculators]({{ '/calculators/' | relative_url }}) and a [finance glossary]({{ '/glossary/' | relative_url }}).
